## What I got wrong at the start?

Initially, I was thinking of making a more robust web application that records the in and out timestamps for students whenever they walk in and out of the hostel. My initial commit is a proof to that initial plan. Moreover, in order to improve authenticity I was thinking about linking each student with their RegistrationID, where each student can only record in or out coming from their own phone registered with their ID

However, this meant infrastructural changes. My aim was not to create a hostel attendance management app, but simply to help Ms Babita Kumari, who strugles every night to scan through an array of rows filled with messy and inconsistent data. The main aim of this project is to save 30 minutes from and her and keep the facts in an organised manner that can be easily accessed. I prioritised solving her problem over correcting the hostel attendance tracker system simply because today morning when I went out looking for a real problem faced by a staff or anybody else in the neighborhood, this problem looked like a geunine one that can be easily improvised. This alligns perfectly with the goal of this aasignment.
